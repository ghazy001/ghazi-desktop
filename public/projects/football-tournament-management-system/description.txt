Engineering considerations
--------------------------
Keep competition concepts consistent.
Teams, matches, and schedules need consistent meaning across clients. Shared validation rules and data contracts are important considerations for multiplatform systems.
Scheduling is more than displaying dates.
Automated scheduling introduces constraints such as team availability and conflicting matches. Documenting supported rules makes the behavior easier to inspect and test.
Distinguish notifications from stored state.
Notifications tell users that something changed; the application data remains the reference. Reconnect recovery and clear update states are useful review points.
