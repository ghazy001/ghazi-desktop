Engineering considerations
--------------------------
Keep AI supportive, not authoritative.
Emotion detection should be treated as a model inference, not a reliable diagnosis or a definitive assessment of a learner. Clear communication of uncertainty is important for this type of feature.
Design for live conversations.
A WebSocket chatroom introduces connection state, message ordering, and reconnect considerations alongside the regular HTTP application flow.
Treat identity and facial data carefully.
Google OAuth and facial recognition introduce different privacy boundaries. Consent, data minimization, and protection of identity information are important review points.
