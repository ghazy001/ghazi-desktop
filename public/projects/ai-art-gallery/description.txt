Engineering considerations
--------------------------
Separate creative output from analysis.
Generating an image, removing a background, and classifying an artist solve different problems. Distinct inputs and results make these tools easier to understand.
Present estimates as estimates.
An artist classification or price estimate is model output, not authenticated attribution or a professional appraisal. The interface should make that distinction clear.
Consider image ownership and privacy.
Uploaded artwork and generated images introduce consent, rights, and storage questions. These are important considerations for a creative AI platform.
